Tuition Fee Rules & Physical College Reporting

Financial settlement policies and mandatory joining compliance.

Tuition Fee Payment Rules
Minimum Fee Settlement

All candidates must pay a minimum of **₹5,000 (for SC/ST)** and **₹10,000 (for Others)** along with the tuition fee where the allotted net tuition fee payable is less than these respective amounts.

Self-Reporting with Zero Fee

If eligible for full fee reimbursement (displaying Net Fee as ₹0), the candidate must log in and submit the web self-reporting to confirm the seat, otherwise the allotment gets cancelled automatically.

Refund Payment Account Policy

Candidates are strongly advised to make tuition fee payments using their parents' or own personal bank accounts. Any refunds due to subsequent cancellations will be credited back only to the original payment source account.

Mandatory Physical Joining Protocols
Post Final Phase Reporting

Physical reporting in person at the allotted college is **mandatory** after the Final Phase of allotments. Only performing online self-reporting without visiting the college within the scheduled timeline will lead to automatic cancellation of the seat.

Documents to Handover

Upon physical reporting, the candidate must hand over the **original Transfer Certificate (T.C.)** along with **two sets of Xerox copies** of all verification certificates.

Cancel & Branch Upgrades

If a seat is upgraded to another college in the Final Phase, the candidate must collect the original T.C. from the previous college and submit it to the newly allotted college to secure the seat.
